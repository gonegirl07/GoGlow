# Dance case 001 — repeatable choreography phrase

Status: PLANNED

## Goal
Verify a short phrase with clear rhythm, weight transfer, and a stable ending pose.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Choreography has 2–4 ordered movements.
- [ ] Foot placement and weight transfer are readable.
- [ ] Arms coordinate with steps.
- [ ] No unrequested spins or random choreography changes.
- [ ] Hands and feet remain anatomically plausible.
- [ ] Final pose is stable.
