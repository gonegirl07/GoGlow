# Cycling prompt template

A vertical 9:16 shot of [identity anchors] wearing [wardrobe], riding [bicycle] on [specific road/path]. At the start, [rider/bicycle position, orientation, hand and foot placement]. The rider travels [explicit screen/world direction and path] at [realistic speed], maintaining steady pedaling cadence. Both hands stay on the handlebars and feet stay on the pedals; wheels rotate consistently with forward travel. The bicycle stays on [correct side/lane] and follows [trajectory]. The camera uses [one tracking setup] at [distance/height], moving consistently with the rider. By the end, the rider reaches [end position] and [slows/stops/continues] in a controlled way. Keep anatomy, bicycle geometry, contact points, wheel rotation, shadows, and road direction consistent.

QC: intended direction; wheel rotation and cadence; hands/feet contact; plausible speed; correct lane/path; stable bicycle geometry; end position matches next scene.

Avoid vague “rides forward”, contradictory camera/travel directions, abrupt lane changes, floating feet, impossible joints, sideways wheel sliding, or inconsistent background motion.
