# Yoga prompt template

A vertical 9:16 full-body shot of [identity anchors] wearing [wardrobe], on [mat/surface] in [environment]. Begin in [starting pose and support points]. Slowly transition into [one pose or one specific movement] over [duration], in this sequence: [joint and weight shifts]. Keep [supporting foot/hand], [knee direction], [hip orientation], [spine], [shoulders], and [gaze] aligned. The camera [locked-off/slow deliberate move] keeps the full body and mat visible. Hold the final pose steadily for [time]. Do not add extra pose changes.

QC: pose matches intent; left/right limbs correct; hands/feet grounded; balance and joint alignment plausible; transition readable; full body visible; ending pose stable.

Avoid multiple poses in a short shot, vague “do yoga”, impossible angles, unsupported balance, unintended mirroring, or cropping out support points.
