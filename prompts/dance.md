# Dance prompt template

A vertical 9:16 full-body performance of [identity anchors] wearing [wardrobe] in [environment]. Start with [foot positions, weight-bearing leg, arms, facing]. Perform only this [style] phrase: [2–4 ordered movements] on a steady [tempo] beat. Shift weight from [foot/leg] to [foot/leg] at [timing], keep torso balanced, and coordinate arms with steps. The camera [locked full-body/controlled tracking] keeps hands and feet in frame. Finish in [specific pose and facing] and hold briefly. No unrequested spins, jumps, or choreography changes.

QC: visible footwork; coherent weight shifts; arms match steps; stable anatomy; requested style; explicit turns; clean ending pose.

Avoid too many moves, vague “dance energetically”, cuts hiding footwork, foot sliding, random spins, or sudden facing flips.
